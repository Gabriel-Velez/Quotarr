LIBRARY CLEANUP

Hide media from Quotarr

0 titles

By default, this only hides media from Quotarr. You can optionally remove titles from Radarr or Sonarr, and separately choose whether to delete their files. Quotarr can restore visibility, but cannot undo removal from those services or recover deleted files.

There is no separate delete-enable switch. Radarr/Sonarr must have write and delete permission for the media folder. For a safety net, configure Settings → Media Management → Recycle Bin in each service; without a recycle bin, deleted files may be permanent.
